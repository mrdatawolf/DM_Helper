# The Keep of the Four Worlds

A note for the DM: unlike Amber, Rebma, and the Courts of Chaos, the Keep isn't drawn from Zelazny's novels — it's original to this campaign. Nothing here is "canon" the way the other three pillar shadows are; treat this file as a working draft, not a fixed answer.

## Shadow Overview

The Keep is a nexus shadow: a place where four distinct realities converge at precise geometric angles, and the Keep itself stands at the exact center point where they meet. Its architecture reflects that — stone, crystal, shadow, and living matter blended wall to wall, each quarter of the building answering to one of the four worlds it bridges. Whoever holds the Keep doesn't walk Pattern or Logrus to gain power; they draw directly on the convergence itself, manipulating the flow between the four realities without needing to claim either primal force. That makes mastery of the Keep a third road to real power in this cosmology — one neither Amber nor the Courts fully controls or fully trusts.

## The Convergence and Its Cost

order 33 / chaos 33 / dream 33 — the Keep is the one shadow in the setting built to sit in exact equilibrium, and that balance is not stable on its own. Current corruption note: **reality strain at the convergence point, requiring active management to maintain stability**. Left untended, the four worlds it bridges don't drift apart gently — they tear. That gives a Keep-master constant, immediate stakes: the power is real, but so is the maintenance bill, in a way Pattern- and Logrus-users never have to think about day to day.

## Open Questions (deliberately unresolved)

- **Which four worlds does it actually connect?** Nothing is fixed yet. Tying two of the four to existing campaign shadows (Midkemia and the Soul Realm are natural candidates, given they're already a matched pair) could let the Keep become the place where their mirrored corruption becomes visible and actionable — a control room for the exact problem the players are chasing elsewhere.
- **Who holds it now, if anyone?** An empty or contested Keep is a much bigger hook than an already-staffed one.
- **Does the Argent Refrain reach it?** A third power source that answers to neither Pattern nor Logrus is exactly the kind of thing Corwin's creation — or the original Pattern, or the Logrus — might want to understand, claim, or neutralize before someone else does.

## Things That Might Be Explored

1. **The Vacancy** — the Keep needs active management and may not currently have anyone providing it; the players could be the first to notice it's slipping.
2. **Choosing the Other Two Worlds** — a session or two spent deciding (in-fiction, through play) what the remaining two connected realities are, rather than deciding it up front at the table.
3. **A Rival Claimant** — a House from the Courts, a faction from Amber, or something with no allegiance to either could already be maneuvering to take the Keep before the party does.
