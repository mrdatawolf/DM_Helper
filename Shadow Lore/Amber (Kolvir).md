# Amber (Kolvir)

## Shadow Overview

Amber is not a shadow of anything. It is the one true city, built along the slopes of Mount Kolvir, and every other world — Shadow Earth, Midkemia, Billabong's Veil, all of them — is a reflection cast outward from it. Walk far enough through Shadow in any direction and the world you find yourself in will have started, however distantly, from something Amber's princes carried with them: a face, a memory, a wish.

The city itself sits below the palace, which crowns Kolvir. To the north the mountain drops into Arden, the great forest Oberon's line has hunted and ridden through for centuries. To the west, past the sea cliffs, lies the beach and the descent into Rebma. Above the city on certain nights, when conditions align, the moonlight throws up a ghostly double of Amber in the sky — Tir-na Nog'th, the dream-city, where the future can sometimes be read and the mad sometimes wander in for good.

Beneath it all runs the Pattern: an infinite, spiraling design laid down by Dworkin Barimen in the First Age, walkable only by those of Amber's blood (or those who cheat the requirement through blood, Trumps, or worse). Walking it grants the walker the ability to command Shadow itself — to walk between worlds and shape the path as they go. It is the reason Amber's family is dangerous even unarmed, and the reason every other power in the cosmology watches Kolvir the way sailors watch a storm front.

## The Royal Line

Oberon ruled longest and hardest, and fathered an enormous, fractious family across at least two wives and numerous liaisons in Shadow: Eric, Random, Corwin, Bleys, Fiona, Deirdre, Julian, Caine, Benedict, Brand, Gerard, Llewella, and Flora among the most consequential, with older siblings like Osric and Finndo gone before the family's more recent history. Succession in Amber has never been peaceful — the throne has been fought over, sat on by force, abandoned, and fought over again, and any of Oberon's children (or their children) may still have a claim worth dying for.

## The Argent Refrain

This campaign's defining wrinkle: at some point after the family's wars settled, Corwin completed a second, true Pattern — the **Argent Refrain** — with the Crow, Morrí, paying the price to stabilize it. This is not something the original Pattern or the Logrus asked for, and neither has fully made peace with it. The original Pattern is actively concealing the fact that shadows corrupted by the Argent Refrain's dream-axis (the Soul Realm, Midkemia) are bleeding disorder back toward Kolvir — which means Amber itself, right now, doesn't know it has a second, developing problem on its hands. A DM running scenes in Amber can let that ignorance be dramatic irony: the court can be confident, orderly, undisturbed, while the players know better.

## Things That Might Be Explored

1. **The Silence from Kolvir** — the true Pattern knows about the Soul Realm and Midkemia's corruption and is choosing not to tell anyone. Why? What does it lose if Amber intervenes?
2. **A Second Bloodline** — characters bound to the Argent Refrain (like Lily, with her broken imprint) occupy a strange legal and mystical status in Amber that nobody has had to define yet. Are they family? Rivals? A liability the throne would rather not acknowledge?
3. **Tir-na Nog'th** — the dream-reflection of Amber overhead is already dream-axis territory, adjacent in kind to the Argent Refrain's nature. Does the Crow's influence reach it? Does it reach back?
