# The Courts of Chaos

## Shadow Overview

If Amber is the fixed point Shadow is cast from, the Courts of Chaos are the other end of everything — a realm that doesn't so much have geography as have proximity to the Logrus, the primal, serpentine opposite of the Pattern. The Courts aren't a city in the way Amber is a city. They're tiers and circles of noble Houses built on shifting ground, connected by black roads and rivers that don't stay in one shape for long, closer to the Logrus's heart the further "down" a traveler goes. Dragons, demons, and shapechanged nobility are unremarkable here in a way that would cause a riot in Amber's throne room.

Where Amber has one royal family, the Courts have Houses — Sawall, Hendrake, Barimen, Wyre, Chanicut, and others — each with its own territory, its own loyalties, its own capacity for blood-magic and betrayal. Dworkin himself came from House Barimen before he ever laid down the Pattern, which is a detail the Courts have not forgotten even if Amber mostly has. This campaign already treats at least one House's territory as its own distinct shadow (House Sawall Demesne) — a reminder that "the Courts" is really a federation of dangerous neighborhoods, not a single place a party can simply arrive at and understand.

## The Logrus's Grievance

The Logrus was not consulted when Corwin drew the Argent Refrain, and it has not forgiven the insult. As recorded in the Soul Realm's own lore, the Logrus believes it was cheated — that a second Pattern is an imbalance the multiverse itself owes correcting — and it wants to swallow the Argent Refrain to restore what it sees as the natural order. That grievance is not idle: it's the same hunger that quietly built the wizards of the Soul Realm and may be doing something comparable, mirrored, in Midkemia. Any Chaos-aligned NPC with real influence in the Courts has a plausible reason to want Corwin's creation gone, and a plausible way to justify going after characters bound to it.

## Things That Might Be Explored

1. **A House Takes Interest** — one of the Houses (Sawall is the obvious hook, given its demesne already exists in-game) decides a character tied to the Argent Refrain is either a weapon to be claimed or a debt to be collected.
2. **The Logrus's Patience** — is the Logrus actively working to unravel the Argent Refrain, or simply waiting, the way the Original Pattern is waiting in its own silence? Two ancient powers doing nothing yet is its own kind of tension.
3. **Suhuy, or someone like him** — a Logrus-master figure could offer players real answers about what the Argent Refrain looks like from the Chaos side, at a price that's rarely simple coin.
