# Rebma

## Shadow Overview

Rebma is Amber's mirror, submerged. From the beach below Kolvir, a staircase — the Faiella-bionin — descends into the sea, and for those permitted to walk it, the ocean parts into something closer to thick, breathable twilight than water. At the bottom is a city built to Amber's exact likeness, run in reverse: where Amber's Pattern spirals one way, Rebma's spirals the other, and both are equally real, equally binding, equally lethal to walk carelessly. Visitors don't grow gills or tails here — Rebma isn't populated by merfolk in the storybook sense. It looks like Amber's own people, living in Amber's own architecture, simply relocated beneath the waves by whatever ancient magic keeps the sea from crushing it.

Rebma is ruled by Queen Moire, who did not choose neutrality out of indifference. Generations back, Amber wronged Rebma badly enough that the two courts have kept a careful, unhealed distance ever since — courteous on the surface, unforgiving underneath. Moire extends hospitality when it suits her and shows the family's own stubbornness in withholding it when it doesn't.

## History Worth Knowing

Rebma's Pattern has occasionally served as a second chance for Amber's own family — a place to recover, to walk a Pattern when Amber's was unavailable or too dangerous to risk, or simply to disappear from Kolvir's politics for a while. It has sheltered exiled or wounded princes of Amber more than once, always at Moire's discretion and always at a price the borrower didn't fully see coming.

## Things That Might Be Explored

1. **The Old Wrong** — what Amber did to Rebma has never been fully aired to the players. A DM can decide it, reveal it in pieces, or use it as a bargaining chip Moire finally decides to spend.
2. **The Reversed Pattern** — anything true of Amber's Pattern may be true in mirror here. A working, a bloodline claim, or a corruption that reached Kolvir might show up in Rebma inverted, delayed, or not at all — a useful diagnostic tool for players trying to understand what's happening to the Argent Refrain's shadows.
3. **A Second Refuge** — if the Soul Realm or Midkemia's chaos intrusion ever becomes visible to Amber, Rebma is the obvious place for a character to run to first, and the obvious place for Moire to demand something in return for sanctuary.
