My Story

Before You Create a Character

This campaign runs in the Chronicles of Amber multiverse — a world that plays very differently from standard D&D. Character creation here involves systems you won't find in the Player's Handbook:

  • Blood Purity — royal bloodlines and their mechanical effects
  • Power Imprints — walking the Pattern, Logrus, or Argent Refrain
  • Order / Chaos Balance — a sliding scale that shapes your magic
  • Attribute Claims — Amber's diceless ranking system

Your DM strongly recommends reading the Player's Guide before making any choices here. You can always come back to this once you've had a look.
